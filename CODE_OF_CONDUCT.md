# Community expectations

Be respectful and focus feedback on the work. Welcome questions and constructive
criticism. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing and sharing
another person's confidential information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation to
protect contributors. Report sensitive conduct concerns through a private channel
arranged with the [maintainer](https://github.com/Hatef-Rostamkhani). Do not publish
private messages or personal information in an issue.
